## 2. Short Answers

### (a) Does the stock UI clear the inactive buffer before flipping?
**Yes — the stock UI overwrites/clears the inactive back buffer via synchronous Zico DMA (`transfer_src_overlay` -> `MakeOsdVram`) immediately before flipping `active_bitmap_buffer` (`ROM_VERIFIED`).**
- **Bounded ROM evidence (`0xFC380740..0xFC3810EF`, `0xFC380634..0xFC38073F`, `0xFC183820..0xFC183BFF`)**: Inside `VramTransferManager.c` (`sub_fc3809ae` at `0xFC3809AF`, called on `VTMUnLock` / `ScreenUnlock` `0xFC381127` -> `0xFC380A79` and `"VTM SW"` `0xFC380A47`), the firmware:
  1. Redraws dirty Canon UI windows into the single Canon RGBA source buffer (`0x41D81000`, returned by `0xFC33D2BE`) via callback `*(0x0000D28C)` (`0xFC38056A`/`0xFC380596`) and waits for completion via `GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper` (`0xFC143493` at `0xFC3809DA`).
  2. Selects the **inactive** buffer in `sub_fc380984` (`0xFC380985`, called at `0xFC380A12`): loads `active_bitmap_buffer` (`0x0000D2A4`, `[r5, #64]` at `0xFC380988`), computes `next_buf = 1 - active_bitmap_buffer` (`rsb r0, r0, #1` at `0xFC38099A`), and stores `next_buf` into `next_bitmap_buffer` (`0x0000D2A0`, `[r5, #60]` at `0xFC38099E`) and display descriptor `[r4, #4]` (`0xFC3809A4`).
  3. Calls `sub_fc3806fc` (`0xFC3806FD` at `0xFC380A16`) -> `transfer_src_overlay(next_buf)` (`0xFC380635` at `0xFC38071E`) -> `MakeOsdVram` (`0xFC183A13` at `0xFC3806F2` -> `0xFC183825` -> `0xFC20C6D0` -> `mzrm_sendmsg` `0xFC33262D`), which DMA-converts the Canon RGBA source window (`0x41D81000`) into `bitmap_buffer[next_buf]` and `opacity_buffer[next_buf]`, replacing any CHDK overlay pixels in the inactive buffer with transparent/stock-UI pixels before returning.
  4. Calls `sub_fc38090a` (`0xFC38090B` at `0xFC380A1C`), which writes `active_bitmap_buffer = next_buf` (`str r0, [r5, #64]` at `0xFC38091E`, i.e. `0x0000D2A4`) and commits `next_buf` to the display controller via `BmpDDev_SetBitmapVRAMAddress` (`0xFC183690` at `0xFC38092E`/`0xFC38093E`).
- **Sibling evidence**: `chdk-port/platform/g5x/lib.c:9-19,206-219` reflects this exact two-buffer VTM pipeline (`_transfer_src_overlay(n^1); _transfer_src_overlay(n);` and `active_bitmap_buffer` flip detection).

### (b) What are the addresses of both bitmap buffers on GM1.10C?
- **RAM pointer array `bitmap_buffer[0..1]` (`FINSIG`)**: **`0x0000D2A8`** (`bitmap_buffer[0]` pointer at `0x0000D2A8`, `bitmap_buffer[1]` pointer at `0x0000D2AC`; `reference/finsig_gm110c/stubs_entry.S:188`, `chdk-port/platform/g3x/sub/110c/stubs_entry.S:192`), indexed by `active_bitmap_buffer` at **`0x0000D2A4`** (`reference/finsig_gm110c/stubs_entry.S:186`), matching `chdk-port/platform/g7x/lib.c:263-281` and `chdk-port/platform/g5x/lib.c:6-7,21-25`.
- **DRAM YUV422 (UYVY) framebuffers (`ROM_VERIFIED` in `0xFC18347E..0xFC18381F` and `0xFC380740..0xFC3810EF`)**:
  - **`bitmap_buffer[0]`** = **`0x5FE80000`** (loaded at `0xFC183652` from literal `0xFC1837FC`, stored to `0x0000D2A8` at `0xFC380AFC`; paired with `opacity_buffer[0]` = **`0x5FC40000`** at literal `0xFC183800`).
  - **`bitmap_buffer[1]`** = **`0x5FD00000`** (loaded at `0xFC18365A` from literal `0xFC183818`, stored to `0x0000D2AC` at `0xFC380B02`; paired with `opacity_buffer[1]` = **`0x5FB80000`** at literal `0xFC183804`).

### (c) Which hook method fits best, and what is the exact hook point?
- **Chosen hook method**: **RAM function-pointer patch** at **`0x0000E26C`** (`*(volatile uint32_t *)0x0000E26C`).
- **Comparison against existing `NOOVERDRAW` and `CreateTask` hooking in `chdk-port/platform/g3x/sub/110c/`**:
  1. **Task-level wrapper (rejected)**: `hook_CreateTask` (`0xBFE14A3C`, `reference/finsig_gm110c/stubs_entry.S:203`) only intercepts task creation at boot (`chdk-port/platform/g7x/sub/100c/boot.c:61-100`). Because `VTMUnLock_flush` (`0xFC3809AF`) is invoked from multiple stock tasks (`GuiMainTask`, `task_FaceFrame` `0xFC0BFDE3`, etc.) and from the VSync-scheduled `"VTM SW"` callback (`0xFC380A47`), wrapping a task entry point cannot intercept individual buffer flips inside ROM event loops.
  2. **Instruction-level patch (rejected)**: `transfer_src_overlay` (`0xFC380635`), `VTM_FlipAndCommit` (`0xFC38090B`), and `BmpDDev_SetBitmapVRAMAddress` (`0xFC183690`) reside in read-only flash ROM (`0xFC000000..0xFFFFFFFF`) and use direct `bl` instructions (`0xFC380A16`, `0xFC380A1C`, `0xFC38093E`), which cannot be patched in place.
  3. **RAM function-pointer patch (chosen)**: Under `G3X_GUI_NOOVERDRAW=1` (`chdk-port/platform/g3x/lib.c:8-18`), CHDK's `vid_bitmap_refresh()` is a no-op, so only stock firmware calls `transfer_src_overlay` (`0xFC380635`). Inside `GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper` (`0xFC143493`, called by `transfer_src_overlay` at `0xFC380670`), the firmware reads RAM pointer **`0x0000E26C`** (`ldr r0, [pc, #12]` at `0xFC14349A` -> `0xFC1434A8: .word 0x0000E26C`; initialized to `0` at ROM `0xFCE972C4`) and, if non-zero, calls `blx r0` (`0xFC1434A0`).
- **Exact hook point & mechanism for the build task**:
  - **Hook pointer**: **`0x0000E26C`** (`*(volatile uint32_t *)0x0000E26C`, or via firmware setter `0xFC143481`).
  - **Stack-return interception inside `transfer_src_overlay`**:
    - `transfer_src_overlay` (`0xFC380635`) opens with `push.w {r4-r9, lr}` (`0xFC380634`, 7 regs = 28 B), `sub sp, #60` (`0xFC380638`, 60 B local area), and `mov r9, r0` (`0xFC38063A`, saving the `next_buf` buffer-index argument `0` or `1` in `r9`), then calls `bl 0xFC143492` (`GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper`) at `0xFC380670` (setting `lr = 0xFC380675`).
    - `GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper` (`0xFC143493`) executes `push {r4, lr}` (`0xFC143492`, 2 words = 8 B), calls `GraphicSystemCoreFinish` (`0xFC20C5BF`), and invokes `blx r0` (`*(0x0000E26C)`) at `0xFC1434A0`.
    - When our callback at `*(0x0000E26C)` runs, `GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper`'s saved `lr` is at `sp_entry[1]` (`[sp_entry, #4]`). When `sp_entry[1] == 0xFC380675` (confirming the caller is `transfer_src_overlay` at `0xFC380670`), `transfer_src_overlay`'s saved return `lr` is at **`sp_entry[23]` (`[sp_entry, #92]`)**, with its `+92` byte offset derived as **`8 + 60 + 6*4 = 92`**: `8` B is the `GraphicSystemCoreFinish_helper` frame/`sp` offset (`push {r4, lr}` at `0xFC143492`), `60` B is the `transfer_src_overlay` local area (`sub sp, #60` at `0xFC380638`), and `6*4 = 24` B is the six saved `r4-r9` registers before the saved `lr` (`push.w {r4-r9, lr}` at `0xFC380634`).
    - Saving the original `sp_entry[23]` (`[sp_entry, #92]`) and replacing `sp_entry[23]` with a CHDK post-transfer trampoline causes `transfer_src_overlay`'s epilogue `pop.w {r4-r9, pc}` (`0xFC3806F8`) to jump to our trampoline **immediately after** `MakeOsdVram` (`0xFC183A13` at `0xFC3806F2`) finishes DMA-overwriting the inactive buffer and **immediately before** `VTM_FlipAndCommit` (`0xFC38090B` at `0xFC380A1C`) writes `active_bitmap_buffer` (`0x0000D2A4` at `0xFC38091E`) and flips the display (`0xFC183690` at `0xFC38093E`).
    - **Target buffer index (`r9` vs `next_bitmap_buffer` at `0x0000D2A0`) and register/return invariants**: Because `0xFC38063A` executes `mov r9, r0`, the post-transfer trampoline **must not use `r7`**; it must capture the live `r9` value (the `next_buf` index passed to `transfer_src_overlay` at `0xFC38063A`, captured during the `*(0x0000E26C)` callback before `pop.w {r4-r9, pc}` at `0xFC3806F8` restores the caller's `r9`), or equivalently read `next_bitmap_buffer` at **`0x0000D2A0`** (`0xFC38099E`). **The build should capture the live `r9` value** (when intercepting `[sp_entry, #92]` in the `*(0x0000E26C)` callback) because `r9` is the exact `r0` argument passed to `transfer_src_overlay` across all call paths (including `transfer_src_overlay_helper` at `0xFC381174`, which does not update `0x0000D2A0`) and is captured before `pop.w {r4-r9, pc}` (`0xFC3806F8`) overwrites `r9` with the caller's saved register. The post-transfer trampoline **must preserve `r4-r9`** and **return to the original saved `lr`** (`0xFC380723` in `sub_fc3806fc`, returning to the `VTMUnLock_flush` continuation after `0xFC380A16`) so the following `VTM_FlipAndCommit` call at `0xFC380A1C` still runs.
