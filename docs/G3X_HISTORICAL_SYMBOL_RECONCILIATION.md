## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

This document provides a single, reconciled integration of historical symbol recovery (Worker G7) and cross-version gap analysis (Worker G8). It unifies all recovered symbols into one canonical, machine-readable dataset without duplication, establishes a three-version comparison (**G3X 1.00C | G3X 1.10E | G3X 1.10C**), and enforces strict methodological guardrails against speculative address translation.

### Methodological Guardrails
1. **Regional Address Deltas Are Hypotheses, Not Translation Rules**:  
   Observed address displacements between firmware revisions (e.g. `+0x48` in the `CreateTask` veneer table, `+0x0C` in `InitFileModules`, or `+0x1EC` in file descriptors) represent localized empirical offsets between specific anchor pairs. **They are NOT general mathematical translation functions.** Compiler optimization, dead code elimination, and data-pool shifting introduce non-linear discontinuities throughout Canon ROM.
2. **Prohibition of Auto-Generated Stubs**:  
   **No GM1.10C stub may ever be auto-generated by applying an offset to a historical 1.00C or 1.10E address.** Every GM1.10C address must be independently evidenced from tracked ROM disassemblies or primary documentation.
3. **Rigorous Four-Tag Classification Scheme**:  
   Every entry across all datasets is categorized under one of four mutually exclusive tags:
   - **`VERIFIED_IN_EXISTING_DOCS`**: Address is statically evidenced in tracked GM1.10C ROM slices or verified repository documentation.
   - **`HISTORICAL_CANDIDATE`**: Address was populated during 2015 1.00C research or 2020 1.10E work; valid only as a historical research candidate.
   - **`INFERRED`**: Address follows logically from a verified structure (e.g. veneer target) but lacks full disassembled instruction verification.
   - **`UNKNOWN`**: Address is unpopulated, contradictory, or completely unverified for GM1.10C.

## 4. Key Reconciled Findings

1. **Resolution of Historical `task_PhySw` Anomaly**:  
   Ranma's 2015 workbook assigned `0xFC1288F0` to `task_PhySw` with the author annotation `"Different Disassembly"`. Cross-referencing against Ranma's 2020 patch (`0xFC1283AD`) and the GM1.10C static ROM evidence (`0xFC1283AD`, instruction address `0xFC1283AC` loaded by creator `sub_fc1283d2`) proves that `0xFC1288F0` was a speculative match in the deep keyboard scanning logic. The genuine entry point across both 1.10E and GM1.10C is `0xFC1283AD`.
2. **`InitFileModules` Alignment**:  
   The historical 1.00C address was `0xFC0BD064`. In both GM1.10E (`g3x_110e.patch:9040`) and GM1.10C (Slice S2A `[0xFC0BD1A8]`), the Thumb entry pointer is identically `0xFC0BD071` (instruction address `0xFC0BD070`).
3. **`CreateTask` Architecture**:  
   In 1.00C, `CreateTask` was located at `0xFC31BB7C`. In GM1.10C, the ROM veneer is at `0xFC31BBC4` (`+0x48`), branching to ATCM `0xBFE14A3C`. In 1.10E, Ranma hooked the ATCM target `0xBFE14A3D` directly to implement runtime task filtering.
