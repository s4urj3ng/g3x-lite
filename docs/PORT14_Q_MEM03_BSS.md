## 1. Executive Summary and Epistemic Labels

Every claim in this document is labelled with one of three epistemic classifications `[VERIFIED]`:
- **`VERIFIED`**: Directly proven by tracked repository files (exact `file:line` text, prototype source code, or arithmetic on tracked constants).
- **`INFERRED`**: Derived from tracked prototype models, historical/narrative project reports, or unverified secondary claims without reproducible tracked primary `GM1.10C` ROM instruction disassembly.
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Cannot be decided or verified from tracked repository artifacts alone.

### Summary of Decisions

1. **(a) Static BSS Bounds Reconciliation (`0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` inclusive vs `[0x3F4F4,0x3489B4)` half-open vs `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B4` loop-limit notation)**:
   - **Decision**: The closed/inclusive form `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` (`0x0003F4F4–0x003489B3`) and the half-open form `[0x3F4F4,0x3489B4)` are **mathematically equivalent** representations of the exact same `3,183,808`-byte (`795,952` 32-bit words) memory span `[VERIFIED]`. Within `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py`, the closed/inclusive notation `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` (`tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:331-332, 444, 988, 1004`) is the **canonical** interval convention, while `0x003489B4` is canonically registered as the exclusive upper limit / default DryOS heap base (`tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:333, 449`) `[VERIFIED]`. Whether the `GM1.10C` ROM early-boot zeroing loop itself enforces these exact bounds in hardware/ROM remains **`INFERRED`** (modeled in `chdk-port/platform/g3x/sub/110c/boot_prototype.c:144-153`, but lacking tracked primary `GM1.10C` ROM disassembly of the BSS zeroing loop and literal pool) `[INFERRED]`.
2. **(b) Early-Boot Zeroing-Loop Range Reconciliation (`0xFC020068..78` vs `0xFC020070..0xFC020078`)**:
   - **Decision**: Tracked repository artifacts **cannot decide** whether the `GM1.10C` early-boot BSS zeroing loop starts at `0xFC020068` (`0xFC020068..78` in `docs/PORT3A_RESTART_IRQ_AUDIT.md:20`) or `0xFC020070` (`0xFC020070..0xFC020078` in `docs/PORT13_REVIEW_PR23.md:66, 152-155`), nor can tracked files verify the ROM instructions at either range (`UNKNOWN` from tracked artifacts alone) `[VERIFIED]`. Although both candidate ranges fall inside the address span of pinned ROM Slice `S1A` (`0xFC020040..0xFC0200CF`, `docs/PORT11A_GM110C_ROM_EVIDENCE.md:60`), the tracked `S1A` disassembly table (`docs/PORT11A_GM110C_ROM_EVIDENCE.md:103-110`) only documents the ATCM copy loop at `0xFC020040..0xFC020052` and its literals at `0xFC0200C4..0xFC0200CC`, and `docs/PORT3A_RESTART_IRQ_AUDIT.md:9-10, 20` relies on local-only untracked files (`firmware/dumps/GM1.10C/PRIMARY.BIN` and `tests/test_restart_irq_audit.py`, confirmed untracked at `docs/CODEX_INDEPENDENT_RESTART_REVIEW.md:35, 144, 148` and `docs/PORT11A_GM110C_ROM_EVIDENCE.md:37`) `[VERIFIED]`.
3. **(c) Literal-Reference Subclaim (`0xFC0200DC` / `0xFC05B450`) and Overall `OBLIG-MEM-03` Status**:
   - **Decision**: The literal-reference subclaim (`0xFC0200DC` anchoring BSS zero end `0x003489B4` and `0xFC05B450` anchoring DryOS heap base `0x003489B4`) **stays unproven** (`INFERRED` / narrative-only reporting at `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md:295, 304` and `docs/HANDOFF.md:795, 823`, audited at `docs/PORT13_REVIEW_PR23.md:141-146`), and **`OBLIG-MEM-03` overall stays `INFERRED`** `[VERIFIED]`.

## 5. Formal Decisions and Justifications

### 5.1 Decision on (a) BSS Bounds Form: Equivalent Mathematically; Closed/Inclusive `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` is Canonical in `port13_memory_obligations.py`

1. **Mathematical Equivalence (`[VERIFIED]`)**:
   - In `chdk-port/platform/g3x/sub/110c/boot_prototype.c:144-153` (and quoted identically at `docs/CHDK_PORT1C1_CRITICAL_AUDIT.md:147-156`), the BSS zeroing loop is defined as `[VERIFIED]`:
     ```arm
     ldr     r3, =0x0003f4f4
     ldr     r1, =0x003489b4
     mov.w   r2, #0
     loc_zero_bss:
         cmp     r3, r1
         it      lo
         strlo   r2, [r3], #4
         blo     loc_zero_bss
     ```
   - Both `0x0003F4F4` (`259,316` decimal) and `0x003489B4` (`3,443,124` decimal) are 4-byte word-aligned addresses (`259,316 = 64,829 × 4`; `3,443,124 = 860,781 × 4`), separated by exactly `3,443,124 - 259,316 = 3,183,808` bytes (`795,952` 32-bit words, i.e., `3,109.1875` KiB $\approx$ `3.036` MiB as stated in `docs/PORT13_REVIEW_PR23.md:66`) `[VERIFIED]`.
   - Because `strlo r2, [r3], #4` executes only while unsigned `r3 < r1` (`r1 = 0x003489B4`), the loop writes zero bytes starting at byte `0x0003F4F4` through byte `0x003489B3` (the final byte immediately preceding `0x003489B4`) and halts when `r3 == 0x003489B4` (`docs/CHDK_PORT1C1_CRITICAL_AUDIT.md:156`) without writing to `0x003489B4` `[VERIFIED]`.
   - Byte `0x003489B4` is the first byte of the adjacent region (default DryOS kernel heap `0x003489B4–0x006CDFFF` / `MEMISOSTART`, per `docs/PORT5B_HISTORICAL_BOOT_COMPARISON.md:168-169`) `[VERIFIED]`.
   - Therefore, over byte-addressed memory, the closed/inclusive interval **`0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3`** (`[0x0003F4F4, 0x003489B3]`) and the half-open interval **`[0x3F4F4,0x3489B4)`** (`[0x0003F4F4, 0x003489B4)`, also expressed as the inequality `0x0003F4F4 <= addr < 0x003489B4` in `tests/test_restart_sd_audit.py:301`) are **strictly equivalent**, and the `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B4` / `0x3F4F4..0x3489B4` prose notation in `boot_prototype.c:144` and `docs/PORT3A_RESTART_IRQ_AUDIT.md:20` simply names the start and exclusive stop registers `(r3, r1)` of the same half-open loop `[VERIFIED]`.
2. **Canonical Representation in `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py` (`[VERIFIED]`)**:
   - Across `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py` and `docs/PORT5B_HISTORICAL_BOOT_COMPARISON.md:166-171`, adjacent contiguous memory regions are expressed as non-overlapping closed/inclusive byte intervals (`0x00008000..0x0003F4F3` in `OBLIG-MEM-02` at `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:964`, `0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` in `OBLIG-MEM-03` at `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:988`, and `0x003489B4–0x006CDFFF` in `docs/PORT5B_HISTORICAL_BOOT_COMPARISON.md:169`) `[VERIFIED]`.
   - Accordingly, **`0x0003F4F4..0x003489B3` (inclusive)** is the canonical interval form for `OBLIG-MEM-03` in `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:331-332, 444, 988, 1004`, while **`[0x0003F4F4, 0x003489B4)` (`[0x3F4F4,0x3489B4)`)** is its exact half-open equivalent with exclusive upper limit `0x003489B4` (`tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:333`) `[VERIFIED]`.
3. **Epistemic Status of the Bounds on `GM1.10C` ROM (`[INFERRED]`)**:
   - While the equivalence of the bounds notations is **`VERIFIED`** from tracked prototype source (`chdk-port/platform/g3x/sub/110c/boot_prototype.c:144-153`) and `docs/PORT5B_HISTORICAL_BOOT_COMPARISON.md:168`, whether the `GM1.10C` hardware ROM early-boot zeroing loop itself uses these exact bounds remains **`INFERRED`** (`ProvenanceType.PROTOTYPE_MODEL` / `ProvenanceType.NARRATIVE_SUMMARY` in `tools/port13_memory_obligations.py:440-446, 514-520`) because no tracked primary `GM1.10C` ROM disassembly of the BSS zeroing loop or literal `0xFC0200DC` exists in the repository `[VERIFIED]`.
